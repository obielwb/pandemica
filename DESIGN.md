# Design System: Pandemica Results

Scope: this file covers the results and report world only. That means the page generated by `engine/tools/results_page.py` into `engine/results/index.html` and its pt-BR sibling `index.pt-BR.html`. The website in `web/` has its own Tailwind identity (`web/tailwind.config.ts`) and is not governed here. The two worlds share only the brand colors, Pandemica blue and Pandemica yellow.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calibration Certificate"**

The report reads as a certificate issued by a test bench. The model is the instrument under test and the Campinas bulletin is the reference standard. Every figure is a reading checked against a tolerance band. The page is a single white sheet ruled with hairlines. A heavy ink rule opens it under the title block and closes it above the footer. Colors act as roles, not decoration: blue is the reference, ink is the instrument, yellow is the tolerance, and green and red are verdicts.

The page is dense in the way an instrument sheet is dense. It has a label/value spec grid, a row of three reading tiles (deaths, reported cases, and the paper run as the previous instrument), then numbered figure plates with captions. Readings are mono and tabular. Words are set in Archivo, whose width narrows as the rank of a line rises. The system rejects the dark data-dashboard of tiles and the editorial long-read.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White paper, blue-black ink, 1px hairline rules, and a 2px ink rule at the head and foot of the sheet.
- Color is a role: blue for the reference, ink for the instrument, yellow for tolerance, and green or red for verdicts.
- Archivo at varied widths for words. Geist Mono with tabular figures for every reading.
- Figures are numbered plates ("Fig. N") with a key and a caption. The only tiles are the three reading tiles under the title block, chosen by the user over a readings table.
- Flat throughout. The only lifted object is the cursor readout.

## Colors

The palette is a white sheet, a blue-black ink family, and four signal colors that each keep one job.

### Primary
- **Reference Blue** (`ref`): Pandemica blue (hsl 234 100% 47%). It marks the reference standard, meaning the bulletin line in every chart, the 1× perfect-reading line, and the "2020 target" lines. It is also used for links, the focus outline and the reported cells in the waffle. **Reference Tint** (`ref-tint`) fills the area under the weekly bulletin curve in Fig. 3.

### Secondary
- **Tolerance Yellow** (`tol`, applied as `tol-soft` at 34% alpha): Pandemica yellow (hsl 54 99% 48%). It is used only as an acceptance region: the ±30% band around the bulletin, the 0.7×–1.3× column, and the literature R0 2.5–3 band. The full-strength value appears only as the text-selection background.

### Tertiary
- **Pass Green** (`pass`, on `pass-tint`) and **Fail Red** (`fail`, on `fail-tint`): verdict marks only. They appear on the PASS/FAIL marks, on the readout's Error value (inside or outside tolerance), and as the ring colors of the Fig. 8 fit markers (acceptable R0 or not).

### Neutral
- **Certificate Ink** (`ink`, aliased as `inst`): all body text, the instrument (model) line, the head and foot rules, and the filled headline dots.
- **Graphite** (`ink-2`): secondary text, meaning the lede, captions, notes and chart value labels.
- **Slate** (`ink-3`): tertiary text such as spec labels, table headers, axis ticks and figure numbers. It also draws the dashed paper-run line and the "vs." in the title.
- **Hairline** (`rule`): every divider, grid line and table row rule. **Hairline Strong** (`rule-2`): the readout border and empty waffle cells.
- **Plate** (`plate`): the code block ground. It is the only tinted surface.
- **Seed Band** (`seed-band`, ink at 14%): the spread of the three seeds around the headline run.
- **Place Palette** (`place-home` blue, `place-venue` yellow, `place-school` violet, `place-work` orange, `place-transport` grey): categorical fills that stack infections by place in Fig. 7. The user asked for this chart in color (2026-09-11), so Fig. 7 is the one sanctioned exception to the Reference-Is-Blue and Yellow-Is-Tolerance rules. It is for data only and never appears in UI.

### Named Rules
**The Reference-Is-Blue Rule.** Blue always means the bulletin, the reference standard. The model is always drawn in ink. Never swap them, and never use blue for a model series.

**The Yellow-Is-Tolerance Rule.** Yellow appears only as a translucent acceptance band behind data (plus the selection highlight). It is never used for text, fills of marks, or emphasis.

**The Verdict-Only Rule.** Green and red are judgments. They appear only where a reading has been compared with a tolerance.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (variable, wdth 62–125, wght 300–800), with ui-sans-serif and system-ui as fallbacks
**Body Font:** Archivo
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (400, 500, 600), with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular and Menlo as fallbacks

**Character:** Archivo stays upright and changes width to show rank. It is narrow and heavy at the top, and relaxes to full width in running text. Geist Mono with tabular figures carries every reading, so numbers line up in columns like values on an instrument sheet.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (760, clamp 40–80px, line-height 0.9, 78% width): used only for the certificate title. The "vs." is set at 300 weight, 100% width in Slate. pt-BR renders it as "x".
- **Headline** (700, clamp 22–30px, 1.1, 84% width): figure titles. Each is preceded inline by its mono figure number ("Fig. N", 13px, Slate).
- **Title** (650, 17px table quantity / 15px note heading, 1.2, 88% width): quantity names in the readings table and the method note headings.
- **Lede** (420, 18px, 1.45, max 40ch): the one-sentence statement under the title.
- **Body / Caption** (400, 16px / 14.5px, 1.55): captions run to a 62ch maximum and are set in Graphite.
- **Label** (500, 13.5px, 90% width): the spec grid keys. Table headers use 500 at 12.5px in Slate.
- **Reading, large** (Geist Mono 500, clamp 30–44px, line-height 1, -0.03em): the error value in each reading tile. At 56px it becomes the waffle's big number.
- **Reading** (Geist Mono 500, 15px, tabular): reference, measured and tolerance values.
- **Mono label** (Geist Mono 600, 12px, 0.06em): verdict text in the marks and the readout date.
- **Axis** (Geist Mono 400, 11.5px, Slate): chart ticks. Axis titles and in-chart callouts use Archivo 600 at 12–12.5px, with callouts at 88% width.

### Named Rules
**The Readings-Are-Mono Rule.** Any number that is a measurement, count, date stamp or tolerance is set in Geist Mono with tabular figures. Words are never set in mono, except figure numbers and the code block.

**The Width-Is-Rank Rule.** Higher rank means narrower Archivo: 78% for the title, 84% for figure titles, 88% for quantities and callouts, 90% for spec keys, and 100% for running text. Weight rises along with the narrowing.

## Layout

The page is one column with a maximum width of 1180px, centered, with a gutter of clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px) and 72px at the foot. It reads top to bottom as a certificate:

1. The certificate head is a two-column grid (1.25fr / 1fr, gaps of 32px and 48px). The title and lede sit on the left and the spec grid on the right, aligned to the bottom. The head closes with a 2px ink rule.
2. The reading tiles sit in a three-column grid divided by vertical hairlines: quantity name, large mono error, a "simulated · real" line, and a Verdict Mark (PASS/FAIL for the model, a neutral PREVIOUS INSTRUMENT mark for the 2023 paper run).
3. Figure plates have 40px above them and a hairline below. Fig. 1 spans the full width. The middle figures pair up in two equal columns (40px gap), and Fig. 3 splits into three year panels.
4. Method notes sit in three columns (28px gap). The footer sits above a 2px ink rule and holds the sources on the left and the branch in mono on the right.

Responsive behavior: below 860px every grid collapses to one column. The reading tiles stack into one column separated by hairlines. The readout stops floating and sits under the chart. Charts switch to a narrow layout below 640px of plot width: end-of-line labels are dropped, and keys or inline labels take their place. Below 480px the waffle stacks.

Spacing follows a rhythm of 6, 8, 14, 24 and 40px. Of these, 14px is the inner rhythm of rows and plate heads, and 40px separates figures.

## Elevation & Depth

The sheet is flat. Depth comes from rules, not surfaces. The 2px ink rules bound the document, the 1px hairlines divide rows and figures, and the Plate tint is used only behind code.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Readout lift** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 18px rgba(11,14,42,.08)`): used only on the Fig. 1 instrument readout while it floats over the chart. The shadow is removed when the readout sits in the flow on narrow screens.

### Named Rules
**The Ruled-Not-Raised Rule.** Separation is always a hairline or an ink rule. Only an object that floats over data, like the readout, may cast a shadow.

## Shapes

The shapes are square, with small, strictly graded corners: 2px for legend swatches and waffle cells, 3px for verdict marks, and 4px for the readout and code block. Rows, figures and the page itself have no corners at all. Line keys are drawn as rules (a 2.5px solid or 2px dashed stroke) rather than chips. Chart markers are circles: open circles for sweep points, a filled ink circle for the chosen fit, and a colored ring for a verdict. Cases in Fig. 2 are drawn as open squares, deaths as filled circles.

## Components

### Verdict Mark
A stamped verdict: a mono label in capitals with a 13px stroked SVG check or cross icon.
- **Shape:** 3px corners, a 1.5px border in currentColor, 6px × 9px padding, and a 7px gap between icon and text.
- **Pass:** Pass Green text and border on Pass Tint. **Fail:** Fail Red text and border on Fail Tint.
- **Locale:** the label reads PASS/FAIL in English and APROVADO/REPROVADO in pt-BR. The mark grows to fit the word; letter-spacing does not change.

### Spec Grid
The certificate's label/value list: a two-column definition grid with a hairline above the first row and under every row. Keys are Label type in Slate. Values are right-aligned, tabular, in ink. Rows have 7px of vertical padding.

### Reading Tile
One tile per quantity in a three-up row. The quantity name uses Title type, the error uses the large Reading type (the number the eye should land on), then a Graphite "simulated · real" line and the Verdict Mark. Tiles are divided by hairlines, never lifted or filled.

### Figure Plate
The "Fig. N" number in mono Slate sits inline before an Archivo headline. A wrapping key is right-aligned on the same baseline. The plot follows, then a Graphite caption of at most 62ch that states what the figure shows and how to read its bands. Legend swatches are 18 × 10px tiles (2px corners) for areas, or rules for lines.

### Instrument Readout
The signature component. It is a hover readout that tracks the cursor across Fig. 1. A hairline cursor draws down through the chart, and ringed dots mark the bulletin and model values. The box is 94% white with a Hairline Strong border, 4px corners and the Readout lift shadow. It shows a mono date stamp, then label/value pairs, with values right-aligned in mono. The Error value is colored with the verdict colors. The box is `aria-live="polite"` and ignores pointer events.

### Chart Grammar
- Reference line: Reference Blue, 3px in Fig. 1, 2.6px in Fig. 3, 2px for target lines.
- Instrument line: Certificate Ink, 1.8–2.8px. The paper run is Slate, 1.6px, dashed 5/4.
- Grid: 1px Hairline with crisp edges. Guide lines, such as "calibration ends" and variant seeding, are 1px with a 2/3 dash.
- End-of-line labels: an Archivo callout colored like its series, with the value in mono Graphite below it, spread apart so they never collide.
- Motion: the Fig. 1 lines draw in over 1.4s with `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` after a 0.15s delay, only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

### Code Block
Geist Mono at 12.5px, line-height 1.6, on Plate with a Hairline border, 4px corners and 14px × 16px padding. It scrolls horizontally instead of wrapping.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** draw every bulletin or reference series in Reference Blue (#0019f0) and every model series in Certificate Ink (#0b0e2a).
- **Do** show tolerance as a translucent yellow band (`rgba(244,222,2,.34)`) behind the data, and state the band's source in the caption.
- **Do** set every reading in Geist Mono with tabular figures, and right-align numeric columns.
- **Do** open and close the sheet with a 2px ink rule, and divide everything in between with 1px Hairline (#dfe2ee).
- **Do** number figures ("Fig. N", mono, Slate) and give each one a caption of at most 62ch.
- **Do** use `ink-2` or `ink-3` for secondary text on white. Both clear 4.5:1.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green or red for anything that is not a verdict against a tolerance.
- **Don't** use yellow for text, marks, strokes or emphasis.
- **Don't** lift any surface except the floating readout; reading tiles are ruled, not raised.
- **Don't** use a dark ground. This world has `color-scheme: light` only.
- **Don't** pull the `web/` Tailwind tokens into this surface, or these tokens into `web/`.
- **Don't** extend the place palette beyond Fig. 7, and don't reuse its blue or yellow for anything but the reference and the tolerance elsewhere.
